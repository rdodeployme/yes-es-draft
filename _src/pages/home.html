How it works

Enter raw numbers. YES does the rest.

YES records what is on the bill, the docket or the register, then applies the published factors and formulas, so every figure is calculated the same way, every month.

  • Fleet · fuel card statement38,400 L of diesel purchased
    {{ARROW}}
    becomes 104.5 t CO₂-e Scope 1 emissions38,400 L ÷ 1,000 × 38.6 GJ/kL × 70.5 kg CO₂-e/GJ ÷ 1,000
  • Energy · retailer bills612,000 kWh of grid electricity in Victoria
    {{ARROW}}
    becomes 471.2 t CO₂-e Scope 2 emissions612,000 kWh × 0.77 kg CO₂-e/kWh (VIC, location-based) ÷ 1,000
  • Fleet · fleet register171 vehicles: 45 diesel and 42 petrol passenger vehicles, 18 hybrids, 4 plug-in hybrids, 24 battery electric, 37 diesel trucks, 1 electric truck
    {{ARROW}}
    becomes 15.8% of the fleet electrified(24 battery electric + 1 electric truck + ½ × 4 plug-in hybrids) ÷ 171 × 100
  • Waste · weighbridge dockets3,480 t of waste, 1,290 t of it to landfill
    {{ARROW}}
    becomes 62.9% diverted from landfill(3,480 t − 1,290 t) ÷ 3,480 t × 100The landfill tonnes also give Scope 3: 1,290 t × 1.6 t CO₂-e/t = 2,064 t CO₂-e
  • Water · utility bills19,500 kL of potable water and 3,400 kL of recycled water
    {{ARROW}}
    becomes 14.8% alternative water3,400 kL ÷ (19,500 kL + 3,400 kL) × 100
  • Fleet · travel booking system9 domestic and 1 international flight
    {{ARROW}}
    becomes 10 trips recorded Factor pendingNot yet in the totals. Flights are converted once an emissions factor is adopted.

Example month for a fictional Victorian council, calculated with the YES engine v0.1. Emissions factors are the National Greenhouse Accounts Factors 2024 (DCCEEW). Grid electricity is location-based, by state. Each number is entered once and feeds every figure that needs it: the same diesel litres also give upstream Scope 3 and fleet fuel efficiency.

Reporting rhythm

A monthly rhythm, not an annual scramble

Most figures are reported monthly, while the bills are fresh. Quarterly and annual figures fall due at the end of the quarter and the financial year. Static details are set once and confirmed when they change.

Evidence

Every figure has a paper trail

You send the bill, docket or register extract. YES enters every figure from it and grades the evidence, and a second YES analyst verifies the month before it reaches your reports.

  • A
    Primary documentA bill, docket or certificate.
  • B
    System extract or reconciled internal recordA report from a finance, fleet or contractor system, or a register reconciled to source.
  • C
    Estimate or unsupportedA reasonable estimate, or a figure without a document yet.

Country

YES is built on caring for Country

Every figure we report describes land, water and air in a real place. We treat those figures, and the places they describe, with care.

The name

Yindyamarra is a Wiradjuri word, used with the permission of Wiradjuri language custodians. It is often translated as respect, gentleness and taking responsibility.
