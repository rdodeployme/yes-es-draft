How it is built

Ten category scores, one monthly figure

Each category is scored from 0 to 100 by its own published rule. The Yindyamarra Environmental Score is the average of the categories that have data.

One score out of 100

The average of the category scores that have data, rounded to a whole number. Each category counts equally.

Ten category scores

Energy, fleet, carbon, water, waste, circularity, land, nature, community and governance, each scored from 0 to 100 by the rules set out below.

Four bands

Leading, Strong, Developing and Starting, so the number reads the same way in every report and every month.

Direction of travel

Where you are, and which way you are heading

A single number says where you are. Each report also shows the direction of travel four ways, and how far you have come from your baseline year.

Rolling 12 months

Most category scores use the last twelve months together, so one unusual month does not swing the result.

69.8%Renewable electricity share, last 12 months

Against the baseline year

The last twelve months against the same calendar months of the baseline year.

−15.3%Operational emissions against 2024–25

Comparisons are seasonal. Each month is compared with the same calendar month of the baseline year, so winter is measured against last winter, not against spring. Sample values are from the fictional Demo Shire Council.

Provisional and not reported

Clear about what is confirmed

A score is only as good as the data behind it, so every score says whether it is verified and how much of the picture it covers.

Provisional until verified

A month is provisional until YES has checked its evidence and verified it. Any score that includes an unverified month is marked provisional too.

ProvisionalVerified

Targets need a full year

Land, Nature and Community are scored against annual targets. They stay provisional until there are 12 months of data.

Nature · provisionalMonth 9 of 12

Not reported is not zero

A category with no data is shown as not reported and left out of the score. The score says how many of the ten categories it covers.

Category rules

How each category is scored

Every rule uses figures YES calculates from your raw numbers. Trends are measured against the same months of your baseline year. The rules and weights are part of method v0.1, a draft for discussion.

What the score is not

Self-declared, under a published method

The score is a consistent, open way to track your own performance month by month. It is not a rating that anyone has accredited.

Not an accredited rating

No body has accredited the score. It is self-declared under the published YES method.

Not a certification

A score, a band or a verified month does not certify an organisation, a product or a claim. Verification means YES has checked the figures against their evidence. It is not independent assurance.

Not an offset

The score offsets nothing, and YES figures are not a basis for carbon neutral or net zero claims. Avoided emissions are reported separately and never deducted.

A draft for discussion

This is method v0.1. The rules, weights and default targets are a proposal for discussion and may change before launch.
